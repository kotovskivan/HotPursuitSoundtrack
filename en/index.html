01What it will do

Your songs

mp3, flac, wav, ogg, m4a — as new tracks with their own names, next to the game's soundtrack, not instead of it. Keep the game's tracks or turn them off.

Races & pursuits

The game has two playlists: races and police pursuits. Tick where each song plays.

2010 & Remastered

One app for both versions: it finds the game in Steam or the EA app by itself. Music works the same way in both.

YouTube · YT Music · Spotify

Paste a link to a song, playlist or album — the app downloads it, just like Unbound and Heat Soundtrack.

Restore original

Before the first «Install into game» the app keeps a copy of the game's original music files. Restore original — and the game is back as it was. No Frosty, no keys: Hot Pursuit isn't on Frostbite.

Clean zip

No music and no game files in the zip: the songs are yours, the game files are read from your game.

02You need

  1. Windows 10 or 11.
  2. Need for Speed Hot Pursuit (2010) or Hot Pursuit Remastered — Steam or EA app.
  3. Nothing else: no Frosty Mod Manager, no encryption keys.

03Status

In progress

We've figured out how the game stores music: the core already adds songs to races and pursuits and restores the original — tested on a copy of the game files. The first version of the app is ready — now testing in the game. Release — here and on NFS Own Music.

Offline only

The app changes the game's music files. Before going online, press Restore original in the app.
